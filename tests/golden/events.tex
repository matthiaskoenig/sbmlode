  k_{2} &= 2 \cdot k_{1}

\section{ODE system}

The states change in time with the rates of the reactions and the rate rules:
\begin{align*}
  \frac{\mathrm{d} V}{\mathrm{d} t} &= 0.1 \qquad \text{(rate rule)} \\
  \frac{\mathrm{d} S}{\mathrm{d} t} &= \frac{-v_{\mathrm{J0}} - v_{\mathrm{J1}}}{c} \\
  \frac{\mathrm{d} P}{\mathrm{d} t} &= \frac{v_{\mathrm{J0}} + 2 \cdot v_{\mathrm{J1}}}{c} \\
  \frac{\mathrm{d} A}{\mathrm{d} t} &= \frac{-v_{\mathrm{J1}}}{V} - \frac{A}{V} \cdot \frac{\mathrm{d} V}{\mathrm{d} t} \\
  \frac{\mathrm{d} B}{\mathrm{d} t} &= -0.1 \cdot B \qquad \text{(rate rule)}
\end{align*}

The concentration of a species in a compartment whose size changes is diluted by the rate of the size (SBML Level 3 Version 2, section 3.4.6), the amount it holds is kept.

\section{Events}

\textbf{Event \texttt{E1}} (reset)

\begin{itemize}
  \item Trigger: $t > 5$
  \item Priority: $1$
  \item \texttt{initialValue} true, \texttt{persistent} true, \texttt{useValuesFromTriggerTime} true
\end{itemize}
\begin{align*}
  V &\mathrel{:=} 2 \cdot V \\
  S &\mathrel{:=} 10 \\
  A &\mathrel{:=} \frac{A \cdot V}{V^{\mathrm{new}}} \\
  B &\mathrel{:=} \frac{B \cdot V}{V^{\mathrm{new}}}
\end{align*}

$A$ is converted from the size of $V$ before the event to its size $V^{\mathrm{new}}$ after the event, so that its amount is kept.

$B$ is converted from the size of $V$ before the event to its size $V^{\mathrm{new}}$ after the event, so that its amount is kept.
